# Privacy and security

## Who can see your invoices

Only you. Files are stored in a private bucket and are only served after the app checks that you own the invoice. Asking for someone else's invoice returns "not found".

## Data protection

Row-level security is enabled on every database table as a second layer of protection.

## Uploads are checked

Files are limited in size and checked by their contents, not just their name.

## Error monitoring

If error monitoring is enabled, it records that something failed and the error message. It does not receive the contents of your invoices.

## Your responsibility

Use a strong, unique password and sign out on shared computers.
