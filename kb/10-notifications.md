# Notifications

## When processing finishes

If you keep the InvoiceFlow tab open in the background, you can get a desktop notification when your invoices finish processing, so you do not have to keep watching the page.

## Limits of notifications

These are browser desktop notifications, not push notifications. They only work while the tab is open in the background. If you close the tab, you will not be notified, so check your dashboard when you return.

## Turning notifications on

Your browser will ask for permission the first time. If you blocked it, change the site's notification setting in your browser's settings.
