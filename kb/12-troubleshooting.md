# Troubleshooting

## Fields look wrong or are missing

Extraction depends on the quality of the scan. Blurry, rotated or very low-resolution photos are read less reliably. Retake the photo flat and well lit, or correct the fields on the review screen. Every result can be reviewed and edited.

## An invoice is stuck on Processing

Large batches take a while. Retries run automatically, up to three attempts. If it ends as Failed, delete it and upload again.

## Processing fails for many files at once

The reading service has a daily usage limit. If many files fail around the same time, the limit may have been reached. Try again later.

## Totals do not match the original

Check the highlighted message on the review screen. If the supplier's invoice itself contains an arithmetic error, you can approve it after explicitly acknowledging the problem.

## Only part of a long PDF was read

Only the first two pages of a PDF are read. Split longer documents before uploading.

## Upload rejected

Check that the file is a PDF, PNG, JPEG or WebP and is under 10 MB.

## Reading amounts

Amounts written as 1,234.50 or 1.234,50 are both understood.
