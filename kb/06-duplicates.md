# Duplicate detection

Uploading the same invoice twice would double-count it in your records, so InvoiceFlow looks for duplicates.

## How a duplicate is detected

An invoice is flagged as a duplicate when another of your invoices has the same vendor, the same invoice number and the same total.

## What you see

A duplicate is flagged on the review screen so you can decide what to do. Nothing is deleted automatically. Compare the two, then delete the extra copy or keep both if they really are different documents.

## Scope

Duplicates are only looked for among your own invoices. Other users' invoices are never compared with yours.

## When detection cannot work

If the invoice number was unreadable or missing, the match has less to go on. Fill in the invoice number during review so future uploads can be compared properly.
