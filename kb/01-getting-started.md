# Getting started with InvoiceFlow

InvoiceFlow turns invoice photos and PDFs into clean, checked data. You upload files, the app reads the fields for you, checks that the numbers add up, and shows you only the invoices that look wrong.

## Creating an account

Sign up with your email and a password. You will receive a confirmation email; follow the link in it before signing in. After you sign in you land on your dashboard, which lists all of your invoices.

## Your first invoice

1. Open the dashboard and drag a file onto the upload area, or click it to pick files. You can upload several files at once.
2. Each file appears in the table with a status. Reading takes a short while per file.
3. When a file reaches "Needs review" or "Approved", open it to see the extracted fields next to the original document.

## What happens to your files

Your uploads are stored privately. Only you can open your own invoices. Nobody else can see them, and a link to someone else's invoice simply shows "not found".
