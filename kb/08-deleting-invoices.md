# Deleting invoices

## Deleting one invoice

Use the delete (bin) icon on the invoice's row and confirm. The invoice and its uploaded file are permanently deleted. This cannot be undone.

## Deleting several at once

Tick the checkboxes next to several invoices and choose "Delete selected". You will be asked to confirm. Each invoice and its file is permanently deleted, and the app reports how many were deleted and whether any failed.

## Searching and filtering first

Use search and the status filter to narrow the list before selecting, for example to find every failed invoice and delete them together.

## Before you delete

Export anything you want to keep first. Deleted invoices and their files cannot be recovered.
