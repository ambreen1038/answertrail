# Account and password

## Signing in and out

Sign in with your email and password. Use Sign out in the top bar when you are done, especially on a shared computer.

## Forgot your password

On the sign-in page choose "Forgot password", enter your email, and we will send you a link. The link opens a page where you set a new password. Check your spam folder if the email does not arrive.

## Link problems

The reset link only works from the email it was sent in and may expire. If it does not work, request a new one.

## Confirmation email

New accounts must confirm their email address with the link we send before signing in. If you cannot find it, check spam, or sign up again with a correctly spelled address.
